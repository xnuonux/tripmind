Spring = how tightly particles hold their shape. Damping = how much momentum they keep (near 1 = glide forever). Orbit = rotational pull. Brightness tames blown-out seeds. Group sync = 0 makes each particle drift individually (cloud feel). 1 makes them all move together as one body (HAL "marching band" feel). Containment = invisible sphere that bounces particles back inward. Crispness = how hard particles CRYSTALLIZE into shape during hold — 0 lets them drift and breathe, 1 locks them in rigid.
